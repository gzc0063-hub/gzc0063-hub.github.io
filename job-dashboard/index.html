Priority: the plan in plain words

Everything on this page is ranked by one question: how fast does it get a signed job offer and a route to permanent residence in Canada? Permanent Saskatchewan jobs come first, other permanent Canadian jobs second, everything else after that.

Tier 1 — Permanent Saskatchewan jobs

A permanent Saskatchewan job plus an EPA (the SINP job approval, Employment Offer) gives a Saskatchewan nomination worth +600 CRS points.

His score goes from 471 to 1,071: an instant invite. This is the fastest route to permanent residence without French.

Already in: MNP Senior Agronomist (submitted Oct 14) and Simplot Ag Innovation Specialist, Leader SK (submitted Oct 17).

Tier 2 — Other permanent Canadian jobs

The CEC path. After 1 year of skilled work in Canada outside Quebec he is CEC-eligible at CRS 531 (2026 CEC cut-offs were 507–523). After 2 years, 544.

CÉROM in Quebec can be the way into Canada, but move out of Quebec after 1 year. Do not stay in Quebec for permanent residence.

Tier 3 — USA jobs, then the rest

USA roles, then New Zealand and elsewhere. Postdocs and fixed-term roles sit last: they help towards CEC only, and they do not unlock SINP.

Focus only on permanent jobs in Saskatchewan first. Everything else is a backup, not the plan.

The one question for every Saskatchewan permanent employer: “Will you do an EPA for SINP?”
That answer matters more than anything else right now. Every Tier 1 card below has an “EPA asked?” checkbox — tick it when the question has been put to the employer, and record the answer.

Standing rules

  • Every draft needs exact-text approval from Gourav before it is sent.
  • The thread is read first, every time, so nothing is sent twice.
  • An accepted invite from a target company or university gets a follow-up within 48 hours, asking for a 15 minute chat.
  • Academics are not cold-emailed. Connect on LinkedIn instead.
  • No automation, scraping or auto-apply tools.
  • Profile viewers are checked weekly, on Thursday.

How this page is kept

  • Sweeps run Monday (USA), Tuesday (Canada, New Zealand, global) and Friday (USA, second pass).
  • A find is listed only after the official posting is opened and read. A mirror or a search snippet is not a listing.
  • Eligible means the timing and work authorisation can work. Fit means the content matches. They are never merged.
  • Where a posting says nothing about sponsorship, the label says "sponsorship unstated".
  • Nothing is applied for and nothing is sent without approval from Gourav. This page changes what is seen, not what is sent.
