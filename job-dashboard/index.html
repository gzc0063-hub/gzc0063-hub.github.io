Standing rules

  • Every draft needs exact-text approval from Gourav before it is sent.
  • The thread is read first, every time, so nothing is sent twice.
  • An accepted invite from a target company or university gets a follow-up within 48 hours, asking for a 15 minute chat.
  • Academics are not cold-emailed. Connect on LinkedIn instead.
  • No automation, scraping or auto-apply tools.
  • Profile viewers are checked weekly, on Thursday.

How this page is kept

  • Sweeps run Monday (USA), Tuesday (Canada, New Zealand, global) and Friday (USA, second pass).
  • A find is listed only after the official posting is opened and read. A mirror or a search snippet is not a listing.
  • Eligible means the timing and work authorisation can work. Fit means the content matches. They are never merged.
  • Where a posting says nothing about sponsorship, the label says "sponsorship unstated".
  • Nothing is applied for and nothing is sent without approval from Gourav. This page changes what is seen, not what is sent.
